# SEpedia
[![badge](https://shieldcn.dev/badge/vibecoded%20with-Codex.svg?mode=light&logo=ri%3ABsOpenai&logoColor=ffffff&brand=openai)](https://superintelligence-statement.org/) ![built in Faroe Islands](https://shieldcn.dev/flag/fo.svg)

Ever stared at a component and wondered what builds it, what it builds, or which of your forty-seven mods added it? SEpedia puts a searchable encyclopedia inside Space Engineers, so the answers are never far from the cockpit.

## What does it know?

- Items, ores, ingots, ammunition, tools, consumables, and gas bottles
- Cube blocks and the components used to build them
- Production recipes, their ingredients and outputs, and the machines that can run them
- Planet (and asteroid generators coming soon)

SEpedia builds its catalog from the definitions loaded in your current session, so vanilla content and enabled mods all join the same searchable collection. Filters and linked entries make it easy to wander from a component to its blocks, through a recipe, and onward to the assembler that can make it.

## Getting started

Subscribe to [SEpedia on the Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3784965557) and make sure [Rich HUD Master](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1965654081) is enabled. Press `Ctrl+F1` in game to open or close SEpedia. You can change the binding from SEpedia's Rich HUD settings page.
